Make your conversion rose

Type in your deviation readings and out comes a conversion rose for your boat: true, magnetic and compass on one card, ready to print, laminate and keep at the chart table. A classic deviation table comes too.

Your last set of readings is back, kept in this browser so following a link away doesn't lose it.

1Your boat

2Your readings

For each magnetic heading you pointed the boat at, enter how far the compass was off. If the compass showed less than the magnetic heading the deviation is East; if it showed more, it's West. Typing a minus sign picks West for you. How do I get these?

    Advanced: curve terms

    OmniRose draws a smooth curve through your readings, using the simplest curve that fits them well. More terms let the curve wiggle more; that only helps if you have lots of careful readings.

    4Print it

    Conversion roses

    One page for each degree of variation. You'll find the variation for your area printed in the compass rose on your chart, or on NOAA's variation map (where it's called declination).

    Somewhere else

    From to

    Deviation tables

    The deviation table is the classic chart-table card. The combined table also has compass headings on the right, so you can look up either kind of heading directly.

    Swing record

    Your readings as a table, with what the compass showed and how far each one sits from the curve: a record of the swing to keep with your table. The blank sheet has a row for each heading listed above (every 30° if there are none), to take out and fill in on the swing.

    Print at actual size (100%), not "fit to page", so the rose isn't squashed. The pages fit both A4 and US Letter.

    5Keep it

    There's no account and nothing is stored on a server. This page's link is your saved table: bookmark it, or send it to yourself or your crew. The QR code on every printout opens it again too.
