Make your deviation table

Type in your deviation readings and out comes a tidy deviation table and a conversion rose, ready to print, laminate and keep at the chart table.

1Your boat

2Your readings

For each magnetic heading you pointed the boat at, enter how far the compass was off. If the compass showed less than the magnetic heading the deviation is East; if it showed more, it's West. How do I get these?

    3Check the curve

    Enter at least three readings, or try an example, and your table will appear here.

    Advanced: curve terms

    OmniRose draws a smooth curve through your readings, using the simplest curve that fits them well. More terms let the curve wiggle more; that only helps if you have lots of careful readings.

    4Print it

    Deviation tables

    The deviation table is the classic chart-table card. The combined table also has compass headings on the right, so you can look up either kind of heading directly.

    Conversion roses

    One page for each degree of variation. You'll find the variation for your area printed in the compass rose on your chart.

    From to

    Print at actual size (100%), not "fit to page", so the rose isn't squashed. The pages fit both A4 and US Letter.

    5Keep it

    There's no account and nothing is stored on a server. This page's link is your saved table: bookmark it, or send it to yourself or your crew. The QR code on every printout opens it again too.
